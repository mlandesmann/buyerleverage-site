Main Private Equity Partners

BuyerLeverage is proud to have assembled a great team of institutional and private investors. The two lead institutional investors are Access Industries and Regis Management.

Access Industries

Investing in Innovation and Creating Transformative Companies on a Global Scale

Access Industries is a privately held industrial group founded in 1986. Access manages a portfolio of long-term holdings valued across a diverse range of sectors, including natural resources, media, telecommunications, venture capital, and real estate. The group prides itself on backing transformative, high-growth enterprises on a global scale.

The BuyerLeverage technology and patent portfolio has been one of Access’ core long-term intellectual property assets, underscoring a shared commitment to global innovation and strategic impact.

Regis Management

Independent portfolio management for private clients, foundations, and endowments.

Founded in 2000 by the late Silicon Valley leader and pioneer John Freidenrich, Regis Management, now part of Mercer Advisors, is a multi-billion dollar portfolio manager and investment adviser that builds customized portfolios designed to deliver superior long-term capital appreciation. Regis is an investment office with expertise in marketable securities and private investments.

The BuyerLeverage company holdings belong to one of Regis’ original leading investment portfolios and are part of the investments held by John Freidenrich Family Entities. John worked closely with Mark and his team, contributing ideas, expertise, and encouragement.
